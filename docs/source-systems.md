# Source Systems

## Overview

The Campus Placement Intelligence Platform simulates multiple operational systems that generate placement-related events.

Instead of relying on a single dataset, the project models how enterprise data platforms integrate information from multiple independent applications.

---

## Company Portal

Generates:

- Company registrations
- Placement drives
- Job roles
- Compensation details

---

## Student Portal

Generates:

- Student profiles
- Skill updates
- Placement registrations

---

## Placement Office

Generates:

- Eligibility criteria
- Registration deadlines
- Branch restrictions
- CGPA requirements

---

## Interview Scheduler

Generates:

- Interview schedules
- Rescheduled interviews
- Interview round updates

---

## HR Portal

Generates:

- Offer releases
- Offer acceptance status
- Joining information

---

## Why Multiple Sources?

Enterprise analytics platforms rarely receive data from a single application.

Modeling multiple source systems better reflects real-world data engineering scenarios and demonstrates how Microsoft Fabric integrates data from different operational systems.
